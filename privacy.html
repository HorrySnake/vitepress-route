隐私政策Privacy Policy

版本 1.0 · 生效及更新日期：2026 年 10 月 8 日Version 1.0 · Effective and updated October 8, 2026

1. 适用范围与运营主体1. Scope and operator

本隐私政策适用于 CoherentStudio（独立开发者工作室，以下简称“我们”）提供的 Route iOS App 及官方网站。Route 是连接 Apple 快捷指令与第三方 AI 服务的配置和调用工具，支持自备 API Key、Provider、AI Profile、文本/图片/文件输入、结构化输出及可选备用路由。本政策说明我们如何处理信息，不代替您选择的 AI 服务商、Apple 或网站托管服务的隐私政策。This policy applies to the Route iOS app and official website provided by CoherentStudio, an independent developer studio (“we”). Route connects Apple Shortcuts to third-party AI services through your own API keys, providers, AI profiles, text/image/file inputs, structured output and optional fallback routes. This policy describes our processing and does not replace the policies of your AI provider, Apple or the website host.

2. 信息处理概览2. Overview of information processing

Route 无需注册我们的账号。当前版本不通过开发者服务器中转 AI 请求，也未接入广告、跨应用跟踪或第三方行为分析服务。配置、密钥和用量统计主要保存在您的设备；您执行 AI 请求时，所选服务商仍会收到相关输入、认证信息和网络请求信息。“本地保存”不代表第三方不接收或不保存数据。Route does not require an account with us. The current version does not relay AI requests through developer servers and has no advertising, cross-app tracking or third-party behavioral analytics. Configuration, keys and usage records are primarily stored on your device. When you run a request, your selected provider still receives inputs, authentication and network-request information. Local storage does not mean third parties receive or retain no data.

3. API Key 与本地配置3. API keys and local configuration

您添加的 API Key 保存在本机 Apple Keychain，用于向您配置的服务端点认证。当前实现采用仅限本设备的 Keychain 保护属性，不通过我们提供的 iCloud 同步功能同步密钥。Key 不写入快捷指令参数或用量统计。Provider 地址、模型、Profile 名称、系统提示词、生成参数、Schema 与外观偏好保存在 App 的本地存储中。请勿在配置名称或自定义地址中填写不必要的个人信息。Your API keys are stored in Apple Keychain on the device and used to authenticate with configured service endpoints. The current implementation uses device-only Keychain protection and does not provide iCloud key synchronization. Keys are not written to shortcut parameters or usage records. Provider URLs, models, profile names, system prompts, generation settings, schemas and appearance preferences are stored locally. Avoid unnecessary personal information in configuration names or custom URLs.

4. AI 输入、输出与网络请求4. AI inputs, outputs and network requests

运行快捷指令或 App Intents、连接测试、模型发现等操作时，Route 会从设备向相应端点发送完成操作所需的信息，包括 API Key、所选模型、输入文本、系统提示词、生成参数，以及您选择的图片或文件。部分服务需要先上传文件或提取内容，再生成结果。接收方也可获知 IP 地址等通常的网络信息。输出会返回给调用方；后续快捷指令如何保存、展示或转发输入和输出，由该工作流及其接入的服务决定。When you run Shortcuts or App Intents, test a connection or discover models, Route sends the information needed for that operation directly from your device to the relevant endpoint. This includes your API key, model, text, system prompt, generation parameters and selected images or files. Some providers require file upload or extraction before generation. Recipients may also receive ordinary network information such as your IP address. Results are returned to the caller; later storage, display or forwarding depends on the shortcut workflow and connected services.

5. 备用路由与自定义端点5. Fallback routes and custom endpoints

如果您启用并配置备用路由，在符合重试条件的失败发生后，Route 可将同一任务输入发送到您配置的其他 Provider 或模型。因此，一次执行可能涉及多个接收方和多次计费。关闭备用路由可避免后续请求使用这些备用接收方，但不能撤回已经发送的数据。自定义 Base URL 的实际运营者由您选择，请在保存 Key 或执行请求前核实地址、可信程度和隐私规则。If you configure and enable fallback, qualifying failures may cause the same task inputs to be sent to additional providers or models you selected. One execution can therefore involve multiple recipients and charges. Disabling fallback prevents subsequent use of those fallback recipients but cannot recall previously sent data. You choose the operator of a custom base URL; verify the endpoint, trustworthiness and privacy practices before storing a key or running requests.

6. 本地用量记录与错误信息6. Local usage and error records

为展示使用情况与诊断调用失败，App 在本地记录时间、Profile 与 Provider 标识及名称、模型、Token 数（服务商返回时）、耗时、成功状态、路由尝试及错误说明。App 不主动将请求正文或输出正文加入用量记录，并对已知敏感值执行脱敏；第三方错误内容仍可能包含上下文，因此不要直接公开完整错误信息。当前版本保留最近最多 5,000 条详细记录，日汇总在后续记录写入时清理超过约 400 天的条目；累计汇总和本地恢复备份可能保留更久。这些限制不是第三方的数据保留期限。To display usage and diagnose failures, the app locally records time, profile/provider identifiers and names, model, provider-reported token counts, duration, success status, route attempts and error descriptions. It does not intentionally add input/output bodies to usage records and redacts known sensitive values. Provider errors may still contain context, so do not publicly share complete errors. The current version keeps up to 5,000 recent detailed records and prunes daily buckets older than approximately 400 days when further records are written. Cumulative totals and local recovery backups may persist longer. These limits do not govern third-party retention.

7. 存储、备份与删除7. Storage, backups and deletion

配置与统计保存在设备的 App 存储区域，并可能包含用于恢复损坏数据的本地副本。系统设备备份可能保留 App 数据，具体取决于您的系统设置；Keychain 密钥的设备限制与普通配置文件不同。您可在 App 中删除 Provider、Key、Profile 与 Schema。删除配置不一定同时删除历史统计或旧备份，卸载也不应被视为撤销服务商密钥的可靠方式。停止使用某个 Key 时，请同时到服务商控制台撤销或轮换它。我们不掌握您设备上的完整数据副本，无法从远端替您删除本地内容或第三方已收到的内容。Configuration and statistics are stored in the app’s device storage and may include local recovery copies. System backups may retain app data depending on your settings; device restrictions for Keychain keys differ from ordinary configuration files. You can delete providers, keys, profiles and schemas in the app. Deleting configuration does not necessarily delete historical statistics or older backups, and uninstalling is not a reliable way to revoke a provider key. Revoke or rotate unused keys in the provider console. We do not hold a complete copy of your device data and cannot remotely delete local content or information already received by third parties.

8. 第三方处理与跨境传输8. Third-party processing and international transfers

当前集成可连接 OpenAI、DeepSeek、Kimi、Gemini、Grok 等服务；实际接收方取决于您所选 Provider、地域版本、Base URL 与备用路由。第三方可能在不同国家或地区处理数据，并按其条款决定保留、训练用途、安全审查及删除规则。Route 的请求参数或本地安全措施不构成第三方“零保留”或“不用于训练”的保证。发送他人资料、商业秘密或敏感个人信息前，请确认授权及接收服务的处理条件；需要时先脱敏。Current integrations can connect to services such as OpenAI, DeepSeek, Kimi, Gemini and Grok. Actual recipients depend on the selected provider, regional service, base URL and fallback routes. Third parties may process data in other countries and apply their own retention, training, safety-review and deletion rules. Route’s request parameters or local safeguards do not guarantee zero retention or exclusion from training by a provider. Before sending another person’s information, trade secrets or sensitive personal information, verify your authorization and the recipient’s practices and redact inputs when appropriate.

9. Apple 购买、反馈与网站9. Apple purchases, feedback and website

App 提供自愿的消耗型支持购买，由 Apple StoreKit 处理支付与交易验证。我们不通过 App 接收您的银行卡信息；App 为处理购买读取商品、价格和交易状态，不将 AI 输入或 API Key 附加到购买。反馈邮件仅在您自行确认发送后交给邮件服务和我们，可能包含您编辑的内容及预填的 App 版本、设备型号、系统版本与语言；请勿附带 Key 或未经脱敏的请求内容。我们仅为答复、排查及必要的记录处理收到的邮件，并在目的完成后删除不再需要的内容；法律要求保留或持续处理争议的情况除外。The app offers optional consumable support purchases. Apple StoreKit handles payment and transaction verification. We do not receive card information through the app. Product, price and transaction status are read to process purchases; AI inputs and API keys are not attached to purchases. Feedback is sent to your email service and us only when you confirm sending. It may include edited content and prefilled app version, device model, system version and language. Do not attach keys or unredacted requests. Received mail is used to respond, troubleshoot and maintain necessary records, and unneeded content is deleted after those purposes are fulfilled, subject to legal retention or ongoing disputes.

10. 网站访问与本地语言偏好10. Website visits and language preference

官方网站由 GitHub Pages 托管，不提供账号注册、Key 输入或内容上传表单。网站在浏览器本地存储语言偏好，您可通过清除站点数据删除。GitHub 为提供网站和安全目的处理访问请求信息，包括 IP 地址；其保留与其他处理适用 GitHub 的隐私声明。本站未接入广告或第三方访问统计脚本。The official website is hosted on GitHub Pages and has no account-registration, key-entry or content-upload form. It stores language preference in browser local storage, removable by clearing site data. GitHub processes request information, including IP addresses, for hosting and security under its privacy statement. The site has no advertising or third-party visitor analytics scripts.

11. 您的选择与权利11. Your choices and rights

您可停止运行快捷指令、调整或停用备用路由、删除本地配置、在系统中管理权限，并向服务商撤销 Key 或申请删除其持有的数据。对于我们实际持有的反馈等个人信息，您可通过联系邮箱提出访问、更正、删除、限制处理或其他适用法律规定的请求。我们会在适用法律要求的期限内处理，必要时核实身份，并说明依法无法满足的理由。拒绝可选反馈或支持购买不影响核心功能；停止发送 AI 输入会使相应联网功能无法执行。You may stop shortcut executions, change or disable fallback, remove local configuration, manage system permissions and revoke keys or request deletion from providers. For personal information we actually hold, such as feedback, contact us to request access, correction, deletion, restriction or other applicable rights. We respond within legally required periods, verify identity where needed and explain any lawful refusal. Declining optional feedback or support purchases does not affect core functionality; withholding AI inputs prevents the corresponding network operation.

未成年人应在监护人指导下使用，并满足 App Store 与所选 AI 服务的年龄要求。我们不会明知而要求儿童提交个人信息；如发现相关问题，请联系我们。我们使用 Keychain、系统数据保护及请求脱敏等措施，但无法保证设备或第三方服务绝对安全。政策有重大变更时，会更新日期并通过网站或 App 作出适当提示；依法需要时另行取得同意，不以继续使用代替所有必要授权。Minors should use the service with guardian guidance and meet the age requirements of the App Store and selected AI service. We do not knowingly request personal information from children; contact us with concerns. We use Keychain, system data protection and redaction, but cannot guarantee absolute security of devices or third-party services. Material changes will be dated and appropriately notified through the website or app. Separate consent will be obtained where legally required; continued use does not replace every required authorization.
