隐私政策Privacy Policy

版本 1.3 · 生效及更新日期：2026 年 10 月 8 日Version 1.3 · Effective and updated October 8, 2026

本隐私政策适用于 CoherentStudio（以下简称“我们”）提供的 Route iOS App。Route 是连接 Apple 快捷指令与第三方 AI 服务的配置和调用工具，支持自备 API Key、Provider、AI Profile、文本/图片/文件输入、结构化输出及可选备用路由。本政策说明我们如何处理信息，不代替您选择的 AI 服务商或 Apple的隐私政策。This policy applies to the Route iOS app provided by CoherentStudio (“we”). Route connects Apple Shortcuts to third-party AI services through your own API keys, providers, AI profiles, text/image/file inputs, structured output and optional fallback routes. This policy describes our processing and does not replace the policies of your AI provider or Apple.

1. 信息处理1. Information processing

1.1 Route 无需注册账号。AI 请求由您的设备直接发送至您配置的服务商。API 密钥、配置和使用记录保存在本地，App 未接入广告、跨应用跟踪或第三方行为分析服务。1.1 Route requires no account registration. AI requests are sent directly from your device to your configured provider. API keys, configuration and usage records are stored locally. The app has no advertising, cross-app tracking or third-party behavioral analytics.

1.2 API 密钥保存在本机 Apple Keychain，用于向您配置的服务端点认证。Route 不提供 iCloud 同步功能。1.2 API keys are stored in Apple Keychain on your device and used to authenticate with your configured endpoints. Route does not provide iCloud synchronization.

1.3 API 密钥不写入快捷指令参数或使用记录。服务商地址、模型、配置名称、系统提示词、生成参数、输出结构和偏好设置保存在 App 本地存储中。1.3 API keys are not written to shortcut parameters or usage records. Provider URLs, models, profile names, system prompts, generation parameters, output schemas and preferences are stored locally in the app.

1.4 运行快捷指令或 App Intents、连接测试、模型发现等操作时，Route 会从设备向相应端点发送完成操作所需的信息，包括 API Key、所选模型、输入文本、系统提示词、生成参数，以及您选择的图片或文件。部分服务需要先上传文件或提取内容，再生成结果。1.4 When you run Shortcuts or App Intents, test a connection or discover models, Route sends the information needed for that operation directly from your device to the relevant endpoint. This includes your API key, model, text, system prompt, generation parameters and selected images or files.

1.5 接收方也可获知 IP 地址等通常的网络信息。输出会返回给调用方；后续快捷指令如何保存、展示或转发输入和输出，由该工作流及其接入的服务决定。1.5 Some providers require file upload or extraction before generation. Recipients may also receive ordinary network information such as your IP address. Results are returned to the caller; later storage, display or forwarding depends on the shortcut workflow and connected services.

1.6 为展示使用情况与诊断调用失败，App 在本地记录时间、Profile 与 Provider 标识及名称、模型、Token 数（服务商返回时）、耗时、成功状态、路由尝试及错误说明。App 不主动将请求正文或输出正文加入用量记录，并对已知敏感值执行脱敏；第三方错误内容仍可能包含上下文，因此不要直接公开完整错误信息。1.6 To display usage and diagnose failures, the app locally records time, profile/provider identifiers and names, model, provider-reported token counts, duration, success status, route attempts and error descriptions. It does not intentionally add input/output bodies to usage records and redacts known sensitive values. Provider errors may still contain context, so do not publicly share complete errors.

1.7 详细使用记录会随使用自动更新。日汇总在写入后续记录时清理超过约 400 天的条目；累计汇总和本地恢复副本可能保留更久。相关信息仅用于本地使用统计和故障排查。1.7 Detailed usage records are updated automatically as you use the app. Daily summaries older than approximately 400 days are pruned when further records are written; cumulative totals and local recovery copies may persist longer. These records are used only for local usage statistics and troubleshooting.

2. 第三方服务2. Third-party services

2.1 如果您启用并配置备用路由，在符合重试条件的失败发生后，Route 可将同一任务输入发送到您配置的其他 Provider 或模型。因此，一次执行可能涉及多个接收方和多次计费。2.1 If you configure and enable fallback, qualifying failures may cause the same task inputs to be sent to additional providers or models you selected. One execution can therefore involve multiple recipients and charges.

2.2 关闭备用路由可避免后续请求使用这些备用接收方，但不能撤回已经发送的数据。自定义 Base URL 的实际运营者由您选择，请在保存 Key 或执行请求前核实地址、可信程度和隐私规则。2.2 Disabling fallback prevents subsequent use of those fallback recipients but cannot recall previously sent data. You choose the operator of a custom base URL; verify the endpoint, trustworthiness and privacy practices before storing a key or running requests.

2.3 当前集成可连接 OpenAI、DeepSeek、Kimi、Gemini、Grok 等服务；实际接收方取决于您所选 Provider、地域版本、Base URL 与备用路由。第三方可能在不同国家或地区处理数据，并按其条款决定保留、训练用途、安全审查及删除规则。2.3 Current integrations can connect to services such as OpenAI, DeepSeek, Kimi, Gemini and Grok. Actual recipients depend on the selected provider, regional service, base URL and fallback routes.

2.4 Route 的请求参数或本地安全措施不构成第三方“零保留”或“不用于训练”的保证。发送他人资料、商业秘密或敏感个人信息前，请确认授权及接收服务的处理条件；需要时先脱敏。2.4 Third parties may process data in other countries and apply their own retention, training, safety-review and deletion rules. Route’s request parameters or local safeguards do not guarantee zero retention or exclusion from training by a provider. Before sending another person’s information, trade secrets or sensitive personal information, verify your authorization and the recipient’s practices and redact inputs when appropriate.

2.5 涉及敏感个人信息、向其他信息处理者提供个人信息或跨境传输时，将根据适用法律履行必要的告知、同意及其他程序。2.5 Processing sensitive personal information, disclosure to other controllers and international transfers are subject to applicable notice, consent and other legal requirements.

3. 信息用途3. How information is used

3.1 App 提供自愿的消耗型支持购买，由 Apple StoreKit 处理支付与交易验证。我们不通过 App 接收您的银行卡信息；App 为处理购买读取商品、价格和交易状态，不将 AI 输入或 API Key 附加到购买。3.1 The app offers optional consumable support purchases. Apple StoreKit handles payment and transaction verification. We do not receive card information through the app. Product, price and transaction status are read to process purchases; AI inputs and API keys are not attached to purchases.

3.2 反馈邮件仅在您自行确认发送后交给邮件服务和我们，可能包含您编辑的内容及预填的 App 版本、设备型号、系统版本与语言；请勿附带 Key 或未经脱敏的请求内容。我们仅为答复、排查及必要的记录处理收到的邮件，并在目的完成后删除不再需要的内容；法律要求保留或持续处理争议的情况除外。3.2 Feedback is sent to your email service and us only when you confirm sending. It may include edited content and prefilled app version, device model, system version and language. Do not attach keys or unredacted requests. Received mail is used to respond, troubleshoot and maintain necessary records, and unneeded content is deleted after those purposes are fulfilled, subject to legal retention or ongoing disputes.

3.3 我们仅为提供您请求的功能、处理购买、回复反馈和履行法定义务，在必要范围内处理相关信息；需要您同意的处理，将按适用法律取得同意。3.3 We process relevant information only as necessary to provide requested features, handle purchases, respond to feedback and comply with legal obligations. Consent will be obtained where required by applicable law.

4. 存储与删除4. Storage and deletion

4.1 配置和使用记录保存在设备的 App 存储区域，并可能包含用于恢复数据的本地副本。您可在 App 中管理或删除服务商、密钥、AI 配置及输出结构。系统设备备份是否保留 App 数据，取决于您的系统设置。4.1 Configuration and usage records are stored in the app’s device storage and may include local recovery copies. You can manage or delete providers, keys, AI profiles and output schemas in the app. Whether system device backups retain app data depends on your system settings.

4.2 删除配置不一定同时删除历史统计或旧备份，卸载也不应被视为撤销服务商密钥的可靠方式。停止使用某个 Key 时，请同时到服务商控制台撤销或轮换它。我们不掌握您设备上的完整数据副本，无法从远端替您删除本地内容或第三方已收到的内容。4.2 Deleting configuration does not necessarily delete historical statistics or older backups, and uninstalling is not a reliable way to revoke a provider key. Revoke or rotate unused keys in the provider console. We do not hold a complete copy of your device data and cannot remotely delete local content or information already received by third parties.

5. 您的权利5. Your rights

5.1 您可在 App 中查看、修改或删除配置，调整备用路由，并在系统设置中管理相关权限。如需查看、更正、复制或删除您通过反馈提供给我们的个人信息，请通过联系邮箱提出请求。5.1 You can view, change or delete configuration, adjust fallback routes and manage relevant system permissions. To access, correct, obtain a copy of or delete personal information you provided to us through feedback, contact us by email.

5.2 收到您的请求后，我们会回复并在法律规定的期限内处理。为防止他人冒用您的身份，我们可能请您提供必要的核实信息。如果某项请求无法办理，我们会说明原因。5.2 After receiving your request, we will respond and handle it within the period required by law. To prevent impersonation, we may ask for information necessary to verify the request. If we cannot fulfill a request, we will explain why.

5.3 您可通过联系邮箱撤回基于同意进行的信息处理，或提出隐私问题和投诉。停止相关处理可能影响依赖该信息的功能；不发送反馈或不进行支持购买，不影响 App 核心功能。撤回同意不影响撤回前已合法进行的处理。5.3 You can withdraw consent-based processing or raise privacy questions and complaints through our contact email. Stopping processing may affect features that require that information. Declining feedback or support purchases does not affect core app features. Withdrawal does not affect lawful processing that occurred before withdrawal.

6. 安全与未成年人6. Security and minors

6.1 未成年人应满足 App Store 和所选 AI 服务的年龄要求，并在法律要求时取得监护人同意。请勿在反馈中提供不必要的儿童个人信息；如发现相关信息未经必要授权被提供给我们，请联系我们，我们将依法核实并处理。6.1 Minors must meet App Store and provider age requirements and obtain guardian consent where legally required. Do not include unnecessary children’s personal information in feedback. If such information has been provided to us without required authorization, contact us so we can verify and address the issue under applicable law.

6.2 我们采用 Keychain、系统数据保护和错误记录中已知敏感值脱敏等措施保护信息，并将反馈信息的访问限制在处理所需范围内。请妥善保护设备与密钥，不要在反馈中发送密码、API 密钥或未经脱敏的敏感内容。6.2 We protect information using Keychain, system data protection and redaction of known sensitive values in error records, and limit access to feedback to what its handling requires. Protect your device and keys, and do not send passwords, API keys or unredacted sensitive content in feedback.

6.3 发生或可能发生与我们处理活动有关的个人信息泄露、篡改或丢失时，我们将依法采取补救措施，并按适用法律要求通知相关主管部门及受影响的个人。6.3 If personal information is or may be leaked, altered or lost in connection with our processing, we will take remedial measures and notify competent authorities and affected individuals as required by applicable law.

6.4 政策更新会标明日期。涉及处理目的、方式、信息类别或接收方等重要变化时，我们将以适当方式告知；依法需要重新或单独取得同意的，须在相应处理前取得。不会以继续使用 App 代替法律要求的明确同意。6.4 Updates will show their date. We will appropriately notify you of important changes to purposes, methods, information categories or recipients. Where renewed or separate consent is legally required, it must be obtained before the relevant processing. Continued use of the app does not replace legally required explicit consent.
